The 4 steps

Each step sells something different

The higher you go, the less you sell hours of building and the more you sell diagnosis and accountability. Clients from the lower steps are the same ones who will buy from you at the higher steps.

Step 1 🧱

Fast delivery

You sell what you build

Visual things that are quick to show. Good for gaining experience and landing your first clients; prices fall every year.

  • 🌐 AI-built websites for businesses
  • 🎬 Faceless channel made with AI
  • 📱 AI subscription app (SaaS)
Step 2 🔧

Solve a pain point

You sell a day-to-day result

Customer service, automation, creative assets, AI search. Recurring revenue and an easy-to-show metric.

  • 📞 AI customer service and receptionist
  • ⚙️ Automation and integration
  • 🎨 AI creative studio
  • 🔎 Show up in AI answers (GEO)
  • 🧩 Company AI hub
Step 3 🩺

Teach and diagnose

You sell trust and diagnosis

Workshops, consulting, and lead generation. Pays better and opens the door to large projects.

  • 🎓 AI workshops for teams
  • 🩺 AI consulting and diagnosis
  • 🎯 AI-powered lead generation
Step 4 🧭

Sell your judgment

You sell your decisions

AI director and personal brand. Takes longer to reach, but is harder to replace.

  • 🧭 AI director (in-house or fractional)
  • 🎙️ Education and personal brand
Quick assessment

Which business is your starting point?

Six questions, one answer per question. Nothing is sent anywhere: the calculation happens in your browser.

1. How much time do you have each week for this?

2. How would you describe your technical skills?

3. Do you already have business clients or contacts?

4. Do you have an audience (followers, a mailing list, or a group)?

5. What do you enjoy doing most?

6. How soon do you need to see money coming in?

90-day plan

From zero to your first case study

The plan works for any step. What changes is the offer you bring.

Week 1Choose a business and an industry. Build the demo using a real client’s data, without asking permission.
Weeks 2 to 4Show the demo to 20 businesses in the industry. Land your first client, even at a low price, in exchange for a testimonial and numbers.
Month 2Deliver while measuring before and after. Write a case study showing the number that changed.
Month 3Use the case study to sign 3 more clients and offer the first client the service from the next step up.
The rule that matters most

Choose one and stick with it

People who succeed rarely chose the perfect business at the perfect time. They chose one, stuck with it long enough, and didn’t switch niches every three weeks. Use the assessment to decide, not to put it off.

The prices are reference ranges for what people charge in Brazil (Brazilian reais, R$) in 2026, not a promise of earnings. Results depend on the offer, industry, execution, and time.
